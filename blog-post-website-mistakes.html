5 Website Mistakes That Are Costing You Clients

The small things visitors notice before they've read a single word.

By Webature

Most people decide whether to trust a business within seconds of a page loading — long before they've read a headline or scrolled past the fold. That first impression is shaped almost entirely by things founders rarely think about: load time, layout, and whether the site feels current. Here are five mistakes we see most often, and what they're quietly costing.

1. Slow load times

Every extra second before your site becomes usable pushes visitors toward the back button, especially on mobile connections. Unoptimised images, bloated page builders and unnecessary scripts are usually the culprits. A fast, lean build isn't a nice-to-have — it's the baseline for keeping people on the page long enough to read what you've written.

2. No clear next step

A visitor should never have to guess what to do next. If your homepage doesn't make the path to contacting you, booking a call, or buying something obvious within a few seconds, you're losing people who were genuinely interested. One clear call to action, repeated consistently, outperforms five competing ones every time.

3. Missing SEO foundations

A beautiful site that Google can't understand is invisible to anyone who hasn't already heard of you. Page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure and image alt text aren't afterthoughts — they're what determines whether you show up for the searches your future clients are actually typing.

4. Designed for desktop first

More than half of most businesses' traffic now arrives on a phone, yet plenty of sites are still designed on a large monitor and squeezed to fit smaller screens as an afterthought. That shows up as tiny tap targets, awkward line breaks and forms that are painful to fill in with a thumb.

5. A site that feels dated

Fonts, spacing and imagery all send a signal about how seriously a business takes itself today, not five years ago. A site that hasn't been touched in a while — even if the business behind it is thriving — quietly undermines trust before a single word of copy gets read.

We build every Webature project to avoid all five of these from day one: fast, mobile-first, structured for search, and designed to feel current for years, not months. If your site is showing any of these signs, we're always happy to take a look.
